# Step 2 — Madlen's Unique Value Proposition (draft)

## The UVP (≤ 2 sentences)

> **Madlen is the AI teaching assistant built for your curriculum, not adapted to it: lessons, questions and feedback arrive aligned to your learning outcomes — MEB Maarif, LGS/YKS, IB or Cambridge — and ready to assign. Because planning, assigning, marking and tracking happen in one connected loop, teachers win back hours and school leaders see real learning progress, not just activity.**

Short tagline version (for ads / social): **"Built for your curriculum. Ready for your class."**

## Why this UVP (the reasoning)

**Primary user:** the teacher (daily user). **Buyer:** the school leader (principal / academic coordinator).
- Teachers care most about: time, and output they can trust without re-writing.
- School leaders care most about: measurable learning progress, curriculum compliance (MEB Maarif), data safety (KVKK).

**How Madlen is different — mapped to competitor gaps (Step 1):**

| Rival's gap (evidence) | Madlen's answer (evidence) | UVP words |
|---|---|---|
| MagicSchool: US standards databases, no Turkish UI, machine-translated Turkish output | MEB learning-outcome mapping mode, TYMM, LGS/TYT/AYT, IB/Cambridge, Turkish-native | "built for your curriculum, not adapted to it" |
| Google: asks teachers to refine every output to fit "your context and local policies" | 98.08% of Madlen questions assigned without a single edit (233,702 / 238,281) | "ready to assign" |
| MagicSchool: 80+ separate generators; Khanmigo trial: AI "beside" content went unused ("had to be woven into it") | Plan → create → assign → mark → track → parent/admin report in one system; one tool's output feeds the next | "one connected loop" |
| All rivals sell "time saved" | 308,062 hours saved for 18,566 teachers, *plus* outcome-level analytics for leaders | "teachers win back hours and school leaders see real learning progress, not just activity" |

**What we deliberately do NOT claim:** cheapest (rivals are free), most tools (MagicSchool has more), biggest. We compete where rivals structurally cannot follow quickly: local curriculum depth + a connected loop.

## Proof points (use under the UVP in the deck / doc)
- 18,566 teachers, 150+ K12 campuses (Madlen)
- 308,062 teacher hours saved (Madlen Insight Report, Apr 2026)
- 98% of generated questions assigned without edits (same report)
- KVKK + GDPR, processed in Germany, not transferred to the US (Madlen FAQ)

## Answering the case's own question
> "Is it time-saving workflows? Actionable data insights? Meaningful learning rather than mere automation?"

**Answer: all three, but in a specific order.** Time-saving gets the teacher in the door (every rival offers it). Curriculum-fit makes the output trustworthy enough to use without edits (rivals can't match it locally). The connected loop turns that usage into learning data leaders can act on (the "meaningful learning" proof). The UVP leads with curriculum-fit because that is the part competitors cannot copy.
