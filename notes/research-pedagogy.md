# Research — pedagogy (agent report, 2026-10-05, condensed)

## Lesson Prep
- **Backward design (Wiggins & McTighe):** desired results first, then the evidence, then the activities.
- **Rosenshine (AFT 2012):** review, small steps, frequent checks for understanding, models, guided then independent practice.
- **Revised Bloom (Krathwohl 2002):** objectives use observable verbs.
- **TYMM:** öğrenme çıktıları, süreç bileşenleri, temel kabuller, ön değerlendirme, köprü kurma, farklılaştırma (zenginleştirme/destekleme), öğrenme kanıtları (tymm.meb.gov.tr).
- **Madlen's TYMM blog:** the most common mistake is 3–4 outcomes in a 40-minute lesson. Use 1–2. Madlen's "bağlam temelli" questions need a context that is essential to the answer.
- **Rule:** never invent official outcome codes.

## Student Chatbot
- **Bastani et al., PNAS 2025** (~1,000 students, high school in Türkiye): plain GPT access → −17% once access was removed. A hint-based tutor that was given the correct solution and common mistakes "largely mitigated" the harm.
- **Oreopoulos & Low, NBER w35620 (Aug 2026)** (18 Tennessee schools): hint-only Khanmigo was used on about one-third of practice days. Engagement is "the binding constraint". Khan's response is proactive help and always moving the student forward.
- **UK DfE Generative AI product safety standards (Jan 2026):**
  - progressive disclosure (hints/partial steps first), full solution only after a genuine attempt;
  - no personas implying personhood;
  - filter harmful content, time limits.
- **Sinha & Kapur 2021:** productive failure helps older students but reverses for grades 2–5 → give younger students models sooner.
- **Day et al. 2025:** LLMs hit reading levels inconsistently → use explicit constraints per grade band.
- **Ladder (≤ 4 levels):**
  1. Orient + first move.
  2. Targeted hint on the student's attempt.
  3. Worked step / analogous example.
  4. Full solution only after 2+ genuine attempts, then a "your turn" item.

  Every turn gives a concrete next action, max one question per turn, never 2+ questions in a row without new info.

## Essay Grader
- **MEB ODSGM Ölçme ve Değerlendirme Kılavuzu (2025):** analytic rubric with 4 levels (İyileştirme Gerekiyor 1, Gelişiyor 2, Yeterli 3, Ortalamanın Üzerinde 4).
- **Other rubrics:** 6+1 Traits; IELTS Task 2 criteria (task response, coherence & cohesion, lexical resource, grammar).
- **Hattie & Timperley 2007:** feed up/back/forward, task/process level, never about the person. **Shute 2008:** specific, elaborated, manageable chunks.
- **AI grading risks:** scores compress to the middle (Learning Agency, about 24k essays), name-based bias (Common Sense 2025). Use the full 1–4 range and justify 1s and 4s. No names.
- **Criteria adopted:** Argument & ideas · Evidence & development · Structure & cohesion · Language & conventions.

## MEB AI ethics guideline (TTKB, Jan 2026) / YAZEK
- A teacher declaration is required when students interact with AI (chatbot) or when student work is processed or graded by AI (essay grader). Teacher-only prep (lesson plan) is exempt.
- Principles: disclose AI, AI output distinguishable and correctable by the teacher, human makes the final decision, data minimised and anonymised.
- **Product:** YAZEK reminders on the chatbot and grader, AI disclosure, "draft until approved", no names.
